# Pandemonium

## What does randomization do to this game?

Each level on the map is locked until you find its unlock item. Character and transformation unlocks, weapons, health upgrades and extra lives are shuffled into the item pool.

## What is the goal of Pandemonium when randomized?

Clear The Wishing Engine and watch the credits. What opens The Wishing Engine depends on your goal:

- **Defeat Final Boss:** clear both Shroom Lord and Goon Honcho.
- **Spell Page Hunt:** collect the number of spell pages set in your YAML.
- **Both:** clear both bosses and collect the spell pages.
